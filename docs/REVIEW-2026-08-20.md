# Review — major issues and how to improve, 2026-08-20

A full audit of the codebase against the project's stated goal (an honest
verdict on profitability), plus outside research on what the racing-betting
literature and industry actually do. Every code claim below was verified
against the repo at `file:line`; the load-bearing ones were independently
re-verified. Companion to `ORIENTATION.md` — read that first.

The one-sentence conclusion: **the binding constraint is not the model, it
is the measurement loop — and several of the numbers the project currently
navigates by are measuring the wrong thing.**

---

## 0. Priorities, ranked

1. **Make the nightly record actually run** (GitHub Actions cron, not a
   scheduled Claude session) and **backfill Aug 9–19 from production** —
   ~1,000 scoreable races are recoverable today. (§1)
2. **Change what the record measures.** Bet-level P&L at ~5 bets/day needs
   ~13,000 bets (7–11 years) to certify a realistic 3% edge. Per-race
   model-vs-market log score converges ~500–1000× faster; CLV on recorded
   prices in ~50–200 bets. (§2)
3. **Fix the money-path arithmetic**: the phantom 5% commission, the
   top-pick overconfidence that puts printed floors below fair odds, dead
   heats, the daily-cap dilution by already-run races. (§3)
4. **A pre-race market exists.** SIS prices most Irish meetings; Racing
   Post's GreyhoundBET JSON API covers Irish tracks with forecast prices
   and is reachable from the app's own host. ORIENTATION §4's "no live
   pre-race price feed, and none is expected" conflates Betfair with the
   market. (§4)
5. **Serving/data correctness**: the daily PredictionDataError is
   diagnosed (all-debutant races → empty H2H frame); sectional enrichment
   silently stopped accruing on Aug 1; amendments never invalidate
   materialized features. (§5, §6)
6. **Model upgrades** with literature behind them: conditional-logit
   (softmax) head instead of Platt-then-renormalize, SP-history features
   (currently the deployed model uses *zero* user features), sectionals
   normalized by track medians, ratings with uncertainty. (§7)
7. **Infrastructure debt that can destroy the project**: no DB backup,
   broken fresh-DB migration chain, unauthenticated bankroll mutation
   endpoints, unpinned deps under a pickled model. (§8)

## 1. The verdict machinery has not been running

- **record.csv holds one scored night (Aug 1) in 20 days.** The scheduled
  Claude Routine fired daily since Aug 9 and committed nothing for 11
  nights (the old `nightly_routine.py` was too slow to finish; its
  replacement landed only today). The new `nightly_from_api.py` is
  stdlib-only, ~13s, HTTP-only — a perfect fit for a **GitHub Actions
  cron** that runs it, commits `docs/predictions/`, and *fails loudly*
  (freshness assertion: newest record row ≥ yesterday). An unattended LLM
  session following a 6-step prose prompt is not an execution guarantee;
  keep it as reviewer, not executor.
- **Missed days are lost forever by design.** `main()` scores only
  `day − 1` (`nightly_from_api.py:490`); an unscored CSV is never
  revisited, and a scoring exception even kills the *same run's* sheet
  generation (scoring runs before `build_sheets`). Make scoring iterate
  all un-scored trailing CSVs, wrap it in try/except, and build today's
  sheet first.
- **Aug 9–19 is recoverable.** Production has saved experiment-59
  predictions with `created_at` stamps since Aug 9 (verified via the API)
  plus settled results and SPs. A one-off `--backfill` mode can
  reconstruct sheets from saved predictions, score them with
  `created_at`-based strictness, and mark rows retrospective — ~1,000
  paired model-vs-market observations immediately, at zero honesty cost.
- **Sheets are generated after most races have run.** Today's sheet
  (17:46 UTC) lists 29 bets, 21 on races already finished; Aug 1 (18:07
  UTC) lost 11 of 108 races from the strict cohort. Worse, the daily
  exposure cap was split across all 29, cutting the 8 playable stakes
  ~3.5× below their Kelly size (`nightly_from_api.py:105-141,200-212`).
  Drop already-run races *before* staking, and run the sheet before the
  first Irish off (~09:30 UTC; morning cards start ~11:13 local).
- **The strict cohort keys on the sheet's `generated_at`, not the
  prediction's `created_at`** (`nightly_from_api.py:371-374`), discarding
  races whose predictions were genuinely made that morning by the 11:30
  cron. Carry `created_at` per row into the CSV and use it.
- **The Irish-time conversion hardcodes summer UTC+1**
  (`nightly_from_api.py:356`, `evaluate_bet_sheet_strategy.py:46`). After
  clocks change on 2026-10-25 the strict filter silently misclassifies
  races by an hour. `zoneinfo.ZoneInfo("Europe/Dublin")` is stdlib.
- Smaller scoring-integrity items: one mismatched race id blocks the whole
  day's scorecard (score the matching races, refuse-all only on majority
  mismatch); races whose winner isn't on the sheet deflate log loss (the
  denominator counts races that contribute nothing,
  `nightly_from_api.py:399-414`); no `races_missing_results` column, so a
  night scored on 60/97 results looks complete; scorecards are never
  re-verified after GRI's 14-day amendment window.

## 2. Measure things that can converge

Variance math computed from the project's own Aug-1 settled data (top-pick
mean SP 4.25):

| Endpoint | per-obs σ | verdict on a realistic effect | wall-clock at current volume |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bet-subset P&L (~5/day) | ~1.75 | 3% edge → ~13,000 bets | **7–11 years** |
| Bet-subset P&L | | *falsify the +28.9% backtest* → ~160–260 bets | 1–2 months |
| Flat-stake every top pick at SP (~100/day) | ~1.35 | ±10% ROI → ~700 picks | ~1–3 weeks |
| Paired per-race log score vs de-vigged SP (~100/day) | 0.775 | the 0.144-nat market gap → ~110 races | **~1 day**; a 0.05-nat model improvement → ~9 days |
| Mean CLV of recorded prices (~5/day) | ~0.05–0.15 | 3% edge → ~50–200 bets | 1–3 months |

Concretely:

- **Add market columns to the record.** `score_day` already fetches
  `sp_decimal` in the same responses; it compares only against uniform.
  Append de-vigged-SP log loss, the paired per-race difference, and
  flat-stake-all-picks-at-SP P&L, and persist **per-race rows** (a
  cumulative `races_scored.csv`), not just day aggregates.
- **Start logging prices.** Nothing anywhere records the price a human
  saw or took — the single cheapest unlock in the project. Emit
  `bet_sheet_<date>.csv` beside the markdown with empty
  `price_seen / price_taken / placed / skip_reason` columns; the human
  fills ~5 cells/day; the nightly ingests it into a cumulative ledger
  with SP-at-settlement and an amended-result recheck. Skipped bets with
  the seen price are the control arm — without them the price filter's
  effect is unmeasurable. This unlocks CLV, the industry's fast feedback
  signal (Buchdahl: consistent CLV over ~50 bets ≈ what P&L needs
  thousands to show).
- **Stop peeking at bootstrap CIs.** Re-reading a fixed-n CI as data
  accrues guarantees a false "CI excludes zero" eventually (law of the
  iterated logarithm). Use anytime-valid confidence sequences
  (Waudby-Smith–Ramdas betting CS; ~50 lines of stdlib) with
  pre-registered nulls, one track per endpoint above.
- **Report Benter's ΔR² as the go/no-go number.** The model's worth is
  the pseudo-R² improvement of blend(model, market) over market alone —
  computable retrospectively with SP (measurement, not selection; honest
  under ORIENTATION §8's own rules), and `blend.py` already implements
  the fit. Market R² here ≈ 0.12 (as sharp as Benter's 1986-93 Hong Kong
  public); model ≈ 0.04. If ΔR² ≈ 0, no staking rule can save the
  strategy; if ΔR² > 0, "disagree selectively" has a foundation. One
  number, resolvable from existing data, faster than months of accrual.
- **Stratify by field size and SP band** before reading any comparison:
  uniform log loss is ln(n) (1.79 for 6 runners, 1.61 for 5), so a shift
  in the non-runner mix moves pooled numbers by more than a real model
  improvement.

## 3. The money-path arithmetic is wrong in five places

- **The 5% commission is a phantom.** Betfair has no Irish coverage (the
  project proved it), so execution is at retail bookmakers, which charge
  **no commission on win bets**. Yet `commission_rate=0.05`
  (`bankroll.py:19`) inflates every printed floor by ~5% of (odds−1) —
  fewer fills, directly worsening the sample drought — and deflates every
  measured ROI (the −7% replay included) by ~4–5 points. Meanwhile the
  ledger's own settlement pays *gross* (`api/bankroll.py:99-104`) and the
  backtest double-haircuts (slippage × commission,
  `evaluation.py:174,288`): three inconsistent P&L conventions. Set
  commission to 0 for the bookmaker path (keep the knob for a GB pivot),
  model Rule 4 as a small expected deduction, and prefer Best-Odds-
  Guaranteed books (payout = max(taken, SP) — a free option; verify BOG
  applies to Irish dogs per bookmaker and note it on the sheet).
- **The printed floors sit below true fair odds.** On the only scored
  night, top-pick hit rate came in ~8pts under the model's own
  expectation, and the model's top pick carries a mean +0.144 probability
  gap over de-vigged SP — nearly triple `min_edge`. If rank-1
  overconfidence exceeds `min_edge`, a disciplined human following "bet
  only at/above the floor" takes negative-EV bets on every fill —
  consistent with the −7% replay. Quarter-Kelly caps damage; it cannot
  fix a biased p̂ (Baker & McHale 2013: shrink the probability, not just
  the stake). Fit a rank-aware recalibration on the accruing record and
  require `min_edge` > measured rank-1 inflation. Monitor
  `model_top_prob − devig_SP_top_prob` nightly — it converges in days and
  is the direct dial for this.
- **The "0.02-gross vs 0.05-net" framing in ORIENTATION §5 is false.**
  Since slippage and commission are both 5%, the backtest's gate is
  algebraically the *same formula* as the sheet's floor with 0.02 in
  place of 0.05 — the only real difference is the threshold. Worse, the
  replay arm labeled "backtest (min_edge 0.02, gross)"
  (`evaluate_bet_sheet_strategy.py:122`) is *not* the backtest's rule
  (that's the arm labeled "0.02, net"), so the 98-vs-53 volume comparison
  is attached to mislabeled arms. And min_edge barely binds anyway: the
  top pick clears 0.05 in essentially every race (29/29 today); actual
  volume is set by the price floor at bet time. Decide 0.02, put
  `min_odds_for()` in `ml/staking.py` as the single canonical function
  (it is currently triplicated in three scripts with zero tests), and
  move on.
- **Dead heats settle as full wins** everywhere (`api/bankroll.py:441`,
  the replay, settle-by-position). GRI results contain DH; correct
  settlement is stake/n at full odds. At 3.5–5.0 odds one mis-settled DH
  distorts a week of the live sample. Also: bets settled against
  first-published results are never corrected when GRI amends
  (`reconcile` only touches `pending`), and the replay joins pick to
  result by trap only — a reserve substitution silently settles on a
  different dog.
- **`or 1.0` completeness bug**: `data_completeness` of exactly 0.0 — the
  strongest no-data signal — is staked at *full* weight in all three
  scripts (`nightly_from_api.py:187` etc.); `ml/staking.py` handles it
  correctly. Latent (live values run 0.88–0.97) but exactly wrong when a
  first-time runner appears.
- **Both headline numbers condition selection on SP.** The +28.9%
  backtest's entire profit comes from the SP-clears-floor filter (the
  same report's unfiltered flat-stake arm on identical picks loses
  −7.15%); the −7% "live replay" uses SP to decide *and* settle. That is
  not merely optimistic — conditioning a fill on the closing price
  selects the runners the market drifted against, an adverse-selection
  bias of unknown sign. Treat both as measurements of unimplementable
  strategies; only recorded pre-race prices resolve which way the bias
  cuts. Also, in this market's measured favourite-longshot bias
  (flat-SP ROI by band from the project's own mirror: −14.9% at SP≤2
  worsening to −51.9% at SP>10), the sheet's 2.8–5.5 floors shop
  exclusively in the worst band — test odds-dependent edge floors and a
  max-odds cap.

## 4. A pre-race market exists — the constraint in ORIENTATION §4 is too strong

Verified online today:

- **SIS holds GRI rights to 2028 and sells pricing to operators**, so
  bet365 / Paddy Power / BoyleSports / Ladbrokes price most Irish
  meetings pre-race; oddschecker aggregated 5 of 6 Irish meetings today
  (Enniscorthy, non-SIS, was unpriced). Odds load via JS and scraping is
  ToS-hostile — the existing home-agent + `/api/admin/odds-ingest` path
  (price-source-agnostic by design) fits this exactly.
- **Racing Post GreyhoundBET runs an unauthenticated JSON API** covering
  Irish tracks: meeting lists and per-dog `forecast` (tissue price),
  `chanceOfWin`, `topSpeed`, best recent time, trainer, comments — and it
  responded from this project's US-side egress, so it may not even need
  the agent. First experiment: poll one day hourly to learn when
  `forecast` populates vs off-time. ToS-hostile; keep volume trivial.
- **Zero-scraping fallback**: the human already reads a live price in a
  betting app before every bet — *record it* (§2) and the Benter blend
  can run at decision time with β≈1.12 weighting the market, the
  principled version of the min-price rule.
- **No exchange lists Irish dogs** (Betfair proven in-repo; Smarkets/
  Betdaq/Matchbook checked — UK/AU only), and no turnkey odds API covers
  Ireland. Budget there is wasted.
- **A GB pivot is testable in ~2 days without writing a scraper**: GBGB
  has a JSON results API, and Betfair publishes free daily BSP CSVs with
  MORNINGWAP/PPWAP — *genuinely pre-off prices*. Join them and backtest
  the existing stack selecting against MORNINGWAP: if no edge on liquid
  GB markets, the pivot is refuted cheaply; if edge, the dormant Betfair
  plumbing activates unchanged. This is also the only path that fixes
  sample volume by an order of magnitude.

## 5. The daily PredictionDataError is diagnosed

`compute_h2h_features_batch` returns an **empty DataFrame** when no dog in
the batch has any resulted history (`race_features.py:1963-1964`). The
11:30 schedule job predicts each race individually
(`schedule_service.py:186-193`), so an **all-debutant race** (trials /
qualifiers — 359 of 8,019 resulted 2026 races, 2–10 per card-heavy day,
matching the reported 4–10 daily skips) produces no `h2h_*` columns; the
deployed model was trained with them; `prediction_service.py:547` raises.
Training gives those same races h2h zeros, and the batch by-date endpoint
emits per-entry rows — the per-race path is the odd one out.

Fix at the source: return NaN/zero rows indexed by the requested entries
(matching training: meetings=0, win_rate=0.25 prior), and make the error
message name the debutant case. Two compounding wins in the same area:

- **Batch the schedule job.** It currently re-runs the full ~518k-row
  point-in-time aggregate load and the full ~88k-race Elo walk **once per
  race** (30–60× per day) when `predictions.py:1040-1082` already shows
  the batched pattern; pass `precomputed_features`.
- **Persist skip diagnostics.** `races_skipped` conflates "no entries
  yet" with exceptions, capped free-text only; the nightly prints
  `races_without_predictions` and discards it. Structured per-race skip
  rows would have surfaced this diagnosis weeks ago.

## 6. Data-quality rot that degrades the model silently

- **Sectional enrichment stopped accruing 2026-08-01.** The nightly
  profile job gates on `Dog.sex IS NULL` (`backfill_dog_profiles.py:55`)
  — each dog is enriched exactly once, so every race an established dog
  runs after first sight gets NULL `sectional_time` forever. Early pace
  is the project's own "single most predictive variable". Fix the gate:
  re-scrape dogs with a recent resulted entry whose sectional is NULL.
- **`adjusted_time` is NULL on ~46% of entries** because standard
  ("Sand") going never gets a going allowance
  (`dog_profile_scraper.py:98-104`) — the speed-figure system sees only
  the weather-affected half of racing, with missingness correlated with
  going. Treat Sand as allowance 0.0 and backfill ~240k entries.
- **GRI amendments never invalidate materialized `computed_features`**
  (`feature_store.py:226-242`): the 04:30 re-scrape corrects raw rows,
  serve-time recomputes fresh, training reads stale values. Invalidate on
  `last_scraped_at > computed_at` (plus later entries of amended dogs) or
  drop materialization for training (the deployed model already computes
  on the fly).
- **Reserve substitution keeps the old dog's `days_since_last` /
  `grade_at_entry`** (`db_pipeline.py:231-263` only backfills when NULL)
  — substituted runners, exactly where the market is beatable, get a
  fabricated layoff feature. Scratched runners persist as phantom entries
  and `num_runners` is never recomputed (also wrong place-count in
  settlement).
- **Dog identity is name-only**: GRI's dog id (`gri_id`) is parsed off
  the href and discarded; namesakes would silently merge form histories.
  Zero duplicates among 36,441 dogs today — capture the id before the
  first collision, not after.
- **Parser fragility**: results columns mapped by negative index, no
  field validation, no golden-file tests — one GRI markup change plus the
  14-day re-scrape retroactively corrupts two weeks in one night. Add
  range checks (SP pattern, weight band, position 1–8, time 15–70s) and
  refuse batches over a failure threshold. Also: SP regex misses
  `2.5/1`, backtick and `Q`-prefixed variants (~60 real entries);
  `weight_kg` actually stores pounds; card scrapes return `[]` on fetch
  failure with no retry/log (the exact failure/empty conflation the
  results path was refactored to fix), so a transient GRI outage silently
  drops a meeting's predictions; ~14 zombie/duplicate active tracks
  (including a literal "string" row) get scraped daily; scraped-then-
  dropped fields (seeding → `wide_runner` is constant-zero in two model
  features; per-race trainer; owner/best_time).
- **The data_mirror drops three production columns**
  (`grade_at_entry`, `days_since_last`, `wide_runner`), is 3 weeks stale,
  and load verifies nothing — so "the first honest evaluation" runs on
  systematically different inputs than production. Add the columns, store
  a census with each dump, fail loudly on mismatch.

## 7. Modelling: what the literature says to do differently

- **Train (or at least calibrate) at the race level.** The racing
  literature since Bolton & Chapman (1986) and Benter (1994) is unanimous
  that a race is one multinomial observation: conditional logit /
  Plackett-Luce, i.e. softmax over runners. The deployed stack is the
  known-inferior variant — per-runner binary LightGBM, Platt on
  validation, then divide-by-race-sum at serve
  (`prediction_service.py:583-587`) — and *every calibration number ever
  reported is computed on the un-normalized per-runner probabilities that
  are never served*. The renormalization is exactly what breaks
  calibration across field sizes. Cheap first step: replace Platt +
  sum-division with one softmax temperature fitted by winner-MLE on
  validation races (the machinery is already in `blend.py:120-129` with
  β=0). Full fix: a ~40-line LightGBM custom objective (per-race softmax
  CE; gradient `p_i − y_i` within group — the group plumbing exists in
  `dataset_builder.py:335-338`). Drop `is_unbalance=True` (distorts
  probabilities; a 6-runner race has no imbalance problem). This also
  collapses LambdaRank's three-stage probability pathology (arbitrary
  softmax temperature → Platt → renormalize) into nothing, and feeds
  `race_ordering.py` native strengths.
- **The deployed model uses zero user-defined features**
  (`local_retrain_eval.py:41`, `feature_ids=[]`) — including
  `mean_sp_last5`, the *only serve-safe market signal* (a dog's past SPs
  are the market's memory of its ability, among the strongest predictors
  in this literature) — and consequently most race-relative variants
  never get created. Add prior-SP aggregates (mean/EWM log-odds of last
  5, best SP, SP trend, SP rank in past fields) as builtin features.
- **Feature constructions proven in AU/UK greyhound work**: sectionals
  normalized against rolling track+distance medians (the current
  `early_speed_ratio` divides by the dog's *own* finish time, confounding
  early pace with class); box win% conditioned on vacant adjacent boxes;
  rolling 28/91/365d aggregates over trainer, sire, dam; log prize money
  as class proxy. First-bend leaders win 30–35% vs 16.7% baseline — this
  is the signal family worth the effort. Expect improvement, not
  market-beating: no published fundamentals-only greyhound model
  out-ranks its own market (the project's 31.1% top-pick strike already
  beats the academic SOTA on this exact data source).
- **Ratings**: upgrade `elo.py` (fixed K=24, no decay, no uncertainty) to
  Glicko-2/TrueSkill-style with rating deviation inflated by layoff; the
  RD doubles as an uncertainty feature and a market-free no-bet gate.
  Venn-ABERS probability intervals are the right conformal tool: compute
  the floor from the lower bound p0, gate staking (never the measurement
  record) on interval width.
- **Auxiliary target**: an OOF finish-time z-score regressor feeding the
  win model (the proven two-stage racing architecture); bend positions
  and times are already in the data.
- **CatBoost with native categoricals** (dog/trainer/sire/dam ids) as an
  honest test of entity signal beyond hand features; skip learned
  embeddings and TabPFN at this size (250k rows). Ensemble the existing
  trainers with validation-fitted weights. Expected architecture gains
  are ~0.005–0.02 nats of the 0.144 gap — the gap is informational, which
  is why §4 (a market input) matters more.
- **Fix evaluation hygiene around tuning**: autoresearch accepts/rejects
  up to 100 configs *directly on the final test split* (its "best"
  metrics are selection-contaminated and sit indistinguishable in the
  same Experiments table); Optuna's betting objectives score
  probabilities Platt-fitted on the same validation outcomes being
  scored, while its default log-loss objective scores *uncalibrated*
  probabilities despite the comment claiming otherwise
  (`training_service.py:645-668`). Select on validation (or walk-forward),
  score test once; split each fold's val chronologically for the
  calibrator. Also fit the Henery place/show exponents on the local DB
  (`race_ordering.py:47-48` hardcodes horse-racing constants that
  misprice every forecast/trio the combo Kelly stakes), and refit the
  calibration head weekly on a rolling settled window (the live one is
  5–9 months stale).
- **Devig with power/Shin, not proportional** (`blend.py:65-83`):
  proportional devig ignores the favourite-longshot bias measured in this
  very dataset (148–155% books), so the market benchmark is *understated*
  — the true gap is larger than 1.579 vs 1.723.
- **If ΔR² > 0, the literature's expression for small-field edge is
  exotics** (forecast/trio at tote), where small disagreements compound —
  the PL layer exists; the missing piece is scraping tote dividends into
  results so combos can be backtested with the same rigor. Gate on ΔR²
  first; Irish tote takeout is heavy.

## 8. Infrastructure: three ways to lose everything, quietly

- **No automated backup of the production SQLite DB** — the July audit
  flagged it and promised one; nothing exists. One volume incident
  destroys years of amended results (weeks to re-scrape at polite rates,
  and GRI history pages may not reproduce amendments). Automate the
  existing `/api/admin/backup` endpoint from a scheduled GitHub Action
  into object storage, or run Litestream; verify one restore.
- **`alembic upgrade head` fails on an empty database** (verified:
  `NotImplementedError` at the third migration; and no migration ever
  creates `bankroll_config`/`bet_records` — production boots only because
  seed scripts call `create_all` afterwards). Disaster recovery is
  therefore also an outage with no boot path. Fix the migration, add the
  missing table creations, and add a CI job that migrates an empty DB.
- **Every bankroll mutation endpoint is publicly writable** — config
  (which the nightly reads its strategy parameters from), reset (deletes
  all bet records), settle, delete (`api/bankroll.py:145-192`; only
  `Depends(get_db)`). Anyone on the internet can silently change
  `min_edge` or wipe the ledger, and the tampering would be
  indistinguishable from a rule change. Gate mutations behind the
  existing admin-token pattern and print the config used into the
  committed sheet header.
- **Unpinned dependencies under a pickled model**: the artifact is a
  joblib pickle of live custom classes; the Dockerfile installs from `>=`
  floors with no lockfile; sklearn explicitly does not guarantee pickle
  compatibility across versions. Best case a redeploy crashes prediction;
  worst case it silently shifts the probabilities Kelly stakes are
  computed from. Pin with a lockfile, stamp the artifact with library
  versions + git SHA + train window, refuse on mismatch; longer-term,
  store the booster in LightGBM's native format plus two calibration
  floats. Related: experiment 59 has no `train_cutoff_date` (the
  in-training-window prediction guard never fires for the one deployed
  model) and the artifact is mutable under a fixed experiment id (a
  redeploy swaps the model behind the record's experiment key — hash it).
- **CI doesn't gate the deploy** (Railway ships the default branch on
  push; the workflow never builds the Docker image or migrates an empty
  DB), and test coverage is absent exactly where the open problems live
  (prediction_service, schedule_service, scraper parsers, the nightly's
  identity guard). A train/serve **parity test** — same resulted race
  through `dataset_builder` and `prediction_service`, assert elementwise
  equality — would have caught the H2H skew and several silent
  feature-death bugs (the seeded adjusted-time family materializes NULL
  because the feature-store history loader omits the column its serve
  twin includes, `feature_store.py:115-155` vs `feature_engine.py:47-92`).
- **The frontend money path diverges from the deployed rule**: the
  Predictions page's instant BET/stake verdicts use a client-side Kelly
  with no commission, no min-odds floor, hardcoded config
  (`Predictions.tsx:45-97`) — it prints bets the deployed rule refuses;
  the market-odds field pre-seeds from SP (the one price the project's
  rules forbid informing selection); saving Bankroll settings silently
  resets the running balance to the initial value
  (`BankrollDashboard.tsx:83-103`); and no UI path records a placed bet
  at all (the dashboard's empty state even instructs users to do
  something that doesn't exist). Also: the training-log handler commits
  the training thread's DB session from arbitrary threads via the root
  logger (`training_service.py:165-167` — the likely cause of stalled
  jobs the heartbeat was built to detect), and APScheduler's default
  1-second misfire grace silently drops a day's runs across restarts.

## 9. Corrections to ORIENTATION.md itself

The doc is the project's compass; these claims in it need amending:

1. **"+28.9% — backtest only, not reproduced live"** → understates the
   problem: the number is *unimplementable in principle* (selection
   conditions on SP), not just unreproduced. The same applies to the −7%
   replay (SP decides and settles; adverse-selection bias of unknown
   sign). Neither should anchor decisions.
2. **"There is no live pre-race price feed, and none is expected"** →

## 10. A 30-day sequence

Week 1 — the loop: GitHub Actions cron for the nightly (+freshness
assertion, backfill mode, sheet-before-first-off, drop dead races,
zoneinfo); backfill Aug 9–19; add market columns + per-race persistence;
set commission 0 / min_edge 0.02; fix `or 1.0`; automate DB backup.

Week 2 — the instrument: bet-ledger CSV loop (price_seen/taken/skip);
anytime-valid confidence sequences over the four endpoints; compute ΔR²
and the top-pick gap on the backfilled sample; auth on bankroll
mutations; fix the H2H empty-frame + batch the schedule job (restores
4–10 races/day).

Week 3 — the data: sectional enrichment gate; Sand going backfill;
amendment→feature invalidation; SP regex; parser golden files + range
validation; dead-heat settlement; mirror columns + census.

Week 4 — the model (guided by ΔR² and the gap dial): softmax temperature
head; prior-SP features; track-median sectional normalization; then the
GreyhoundBET forecast experiment and/or the 2-day GB-pivot backtest.

The through-line: **every week of measurement fixes buys more certainty
than a quarter of model work** — and most of the model's supposed −7%
live deficit is currently unmeasurable anyway, because the instrument
wasn't running and the numbers it produced conditioned on a price nobody
can see before the off.
