# Verdict — 2026-08-21

One night of unlimited compute against one question: **is there a strategy
here worth betting real money on?** Everything below was computed tonight
from the production database (2.7&nbsp;GB snapshot, pulled 08:16 UTC with the

## The answer

**Yes — one strategy, narrowly defined.** Win bets only, on the
model+market blend, in ordinary graded races, at mid-range prices, with a
printed minimum price per selection. Everything else measured tonight —
model-only betting, forecasts, trios, thin-form races, short-priced
"bankers" — measurably loses and is excluded.

## The certified rule

Bet WIN on runner *i* when, using this morning's Racing Post tissue book:

1. `p_blend(i) − 1/price(i) ≥ 0.03`, where
   `p_blend = softmax(0.712·ln p_model + 1.120·ln p_market)` — the
   committed artifact's Benter blend;
2. price in [1.5, 12];
3. `p_blend ≤ 0.55` — above this the model is measurably overconfident
   (holdout ROI −6% in that band; the Aug-1 scorecard's 40%+ bucket went
   2/9 — two independent confirmations);
4. field-average data completeness ≥ 0.6 — thin-form races (trials,
   novices) are excluded, see "the leak" below;
5. execute only at or above the printed minimum price
   `1/(p_blend − 0.03)`, at a Best-Odds-Guaranteed bookmaker where
   possible; **no commission** (the 5% "exchange commission" in the old
   configs was a phantom — execution is at retail bookmakers).

Stakes: quarter-Kelly at the tissue price, completeness-scaled, 5%
per-bet cap, 10% daily cap, strongest edges first. ~7 qualifying
bets/day at these thresholds.

## Why believe it

- **The blend beats the closing market itself, out of sample, three
  times independently.** Paired per-race log-score vs de-vigged SP:
  **+0.049 nats (CI +0.007/+0.092)** on 284 live-served races Aug 9–20;
  **+0.037 nats (CI +0.027/+0.048)** on 5,063 normal-race holdouts
  Apr–Aug; and **+0.048 nats (CI +0.043/+0.053)** on 20,539 races over
  19 months of walk-forward refits with every trainer feature removed —
  the edge does not depend on the leak, the artifact, or any one window.
  (The model *alone* still loses to the market, −0.06 nats. Its only
  value is as a market overlay. The old model-only strategy was
  structurally doomed.)
- **The rule's P&L holds under adversarial checks**: settlement-price
  haircuts up to 15% (+27→+35% on the pre-filter arms), lognormal
  tissue-grade noise on the market input at σ=0.30 (+19%, CI floor
  +10%), month-by-month stability (two flat months, three strong, none
  catastrophic), and SP-band consistency.
- **The tissue price feed exists and works.** ORIENTATION §4's "no live
  pre-race price feed, and none is expected" was wrong — Racing Post's
  GreyhoundBET API serves per-dog tissue prices for all nine of today's
  Irish meetings, pre-race, reachable from the production host. 83/83 of
  today's cards captured this morning; 80 joined to our races by
  dog-name matching with zero ambiguity.

## Honest caveats, in order of size

1. **The P&L edge could be small.** The trainer-free 19-month walk-forward
   — the strictest, most conservative construction — puts the rule at
   +3.9% with a CI touching zero ([−1.1%, +8.8%]). The deployed artifact
   should do better than that handicapped bound (legitimate trainer
   signal, fresher training), and its own two estimates say +9.8% and
   +13.2% — but the honest range runs from ~breakeven to ~+15%, and only
   the live log narrows it. Stakes are sized accordingly (€9.52 total
   tonight at the €100 basis).
2. **Selection used SP; deployment uses tissue + your app price.** The
   min-price floor keeps every executed bet inside the certified region,
   and the noise test bounds the input-quality risk, but the first weeks
   of the live log are the real test.
3. **~5 bets/day cannot prove ±10% quickly.** The bet-level record needs
   months. The per-race blend-vs-market log score (the number that
   certifies the engine) converges in days and must be tracked nightly.

## The leak found tonight (and why old numbers were too good)

Every `trainer_*` feature is contaminated by **survivorship
time-travel**: trainer attribution is a static column filled by profile
scrapes, dogs that went on to have careers got profiled, dogs that
vanished after early losses didn't — so in thin-form races the feature
quietly encodes the future. Winner-has-max rate on `trainer_runs_90d` in
those races: 66% vs 17% chance; backtests there showed impossible 96%
win rates. All certification above excludes that population, and a
retrain without trainer features confirms the clean signal
(walk-forward, 2025-01→2026-08: see `docs/predictions/` artifacts).
**Any historical backtest of this repo that included thin-form races is
inflated. The +28.9% number is dead for a second, independent reason.**

Tonight's other permanent findings: Henery ordering exponents fitted on
76,081 Irish races (α=0.966, β=0.674 — the hardcoded 0.81/0.65 were
wrong for 2nd place); the all-debutant PredictionDataError fixed
(neutral H2H rows — all 83 of today's races predict, 22s batched);
dividends and results are scrapeable per meeting from
`greyhoundbet.racingpost.com/results/blocks.sd?...&blocks=meetingHeader%2Cresults-meeting-pager%2Clist`.
